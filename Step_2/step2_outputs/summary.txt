Reading the floor: a storm cell can act as the mirror where its
top exceeds the floor. Typical monsoon cell tops (literature-
typical values, not measured here): moderate ~10-12 km MSL,
strong organized ~13-16 km MSL. Floors under ~8 km: routinely
workable. 8-12 km: needs vigorous convection. >16 km: no.
